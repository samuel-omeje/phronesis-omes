About Phronesis Omes

Thoughtful solutions.
Better spaces.

Phronesis Omes is a Nigerian multi-service company bringing design, construction, property improvement, technical installation and event solutions together.

Who We Are

Bringing connected services under one roof.

We work with individuals, homeowners, businesses, organisations and event organisers seeking practical and considered solutions for their spaces and projects.

Our service areas cover design, construction and renovation, furniture and woodwork, technical installations, and events and stage design.

Headquartered in Enugu, Phronesis Omes serves clients across Nigeria. Project scope, location and delivery arrangements are discussed with each client.
